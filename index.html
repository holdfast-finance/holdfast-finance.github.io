ALL-IN-ONE

Your whole financial life, self-custodied.

  • Banks & cards

    Plaid with your own keys, or SimpleFIN. Sync health you can actually see, and full history imported from statements.

  • Crypto & cold storage

    Exchanges via read-only keys. Ledger, Trezor, Coldcard, Jade and Keystone over USB, QR or microSD. Keys never touch the server.

  • Forecasts that show their work

    Cash-flow bands, what-if scenarios and published backtest accuracy. Free, not a premium tier.

  • Private AI advisor

    Bring your own model: Claude, a local model via Ollama, or any compatible endpoint. Redacted, read-only, every tool call visible.

  • Taxes, year-round

    Optional self-host module: relief scanner, crypto cost basis per wallet, 1099-DA reconciliation and a filing-ready package.

  • Runs on your metal

    Docker on a home server, Unraid or Umbrel. Private behind Tailscale on your own domain. Installable on your phone.
